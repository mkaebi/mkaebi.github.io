I am a PhD candidate in Finance at Insper, in Brazil, supported by a fellowship from the São Paulo Research Foundation (FAPESP) and by the XX Anbima Capital Markets Prize. I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Economics from Insper.

I am currently a visiting researcher at the Financial Markets Group, the finance research centre at the London School of Economics and Political Science. I also work as a research assistant at Insper's [Finance and Macroeconomics Centre](https://cefim-insper.github.io/).

My research interests lie within the fields of empirical asset pricing and international finance. In particular,  I focus on the study of foreign exchange market dynamics, identifying novel common risk factors that price the cross-section of currency returns and on analysing how fundamental economic variables can shape currency investment decisions.
